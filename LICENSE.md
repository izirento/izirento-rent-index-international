## Source statements

1. Rent price change figures: Source: Eurostat, HICP annual data (prc_hicp_aind) and monthly data (prc_hicp_manr), item CP041. Eurostat permits reuse for commercial and non-commercial purposes provided the source is acknowledged (https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/main/help/copyright-notice).
2. Rules and guidance: summarised in our own words from the official legal portals, government service pages and statistics offices named below. Texts of laws are cited by article and linked, not reproduced. Each source is subject to its own terms; see the link in the CSV row.

   - Germany: Eurostat; Federal Ministry of Justice / juris
   - France: Eurostat; Service-Public (Prime Minister / DILA)
   - Spain: Boletín Oficial del Estado (BOE); Eurostat
   - Italy: Eurostat; Gazzetta Ufficiale; Parlamento italiano; Parlamento italiano; Gazzetta Ufficiale

   - Ireland: Eurostat; Residential Tenancies Board (RTB)

   - Saudi Arabia: Real Estate General Authority (REGA), Saudi Arabia
